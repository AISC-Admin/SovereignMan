Capabilities

Areas of Intervention

A unique foundation combining human-driven intelligence powered by open sources, financial forensics and technical development — to produce actionable intelligence, not raw data.

01 // OSINT

Open Source Intelligence

Collection, correlation and analysis of open sources on an international scale.

  • Mapping the digital footprint of individuals, entities and organizations
  • Social media monitoring and threat detection
  • Dark web / deep web monitoring: data leaks, black markets, targeted mentions
  • Identity verification, reputation checks and pre-employment screening
  • Geospatial analysis and image metadata exploitation (GEOINT / IMINT)
02 // FININT

Financial Analysis & Forensics

Unravelling complex financial arrangements and cross-border flows.

  • Analysis of financial schemes, offshore structures and shell companies
  • Fraud detection, anti-money laundering (AML) and corruption cases
  • Asset tracing and reconstruction of financial flows
  • Financial due diligence for investment, M&A and pre-litigation
  • Cryptocurrency analysis, blockchain forensics and KYC/KYB support
03 // CYBINT

Cyber-Investigation

Digital investigations and technical monitoring of cyber threats.

  • Digital forensics and incident reconstruction
  • Detection of identity theft and online fraud
  • Tracking malicious infrastructure (domains, IPs, C2 servers)
  • Technical attribution of attacks and malware campaign analysis
  • Mapping cybercriminal ecosystems and their financial channels
04 // GOV

Law Enforcement & Governments

Operational and technical support for sovereign missions.

  • Support for judicial and administrative investigations under lawful mandate
  • Production of actionable, court-admissible intelligence reports
  • OSINT training and skills transfer for specialized units
  • Cooperation on cross-border cases (organized crime, fraud, cybercrime)
  • Geopolitical and security risk analysis
05 // CORP

Private Investigators & Corporations

Research and verification for private-sector actors.

  • Locating individuals and assets
  • Unfair competition, industrial espionage and internal leak investigations
  • Reputation protection and digital crisis management
  • Verification of business partners and counterparties
  • Documented support for civil and commercial proceedings
06 // DEV

Development & Cybersecurity

The tools that make intelligence operational.

  • Custom application and monitoring platform development
  • Internal OSINT aggregation and visualization tools
  • Secure and encrypted communication solutions
  • Security audits, cybersecurity consulting and system hardening
  • Technology watch and integration of specialized data sources
