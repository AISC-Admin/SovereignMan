About SovereignMan

SovereignMan OÜ is an Estonian company specialized in open-source intelligence (OSINT), financial investigation and secure application development. Based in Tallinn, it operates for institutional and private clients across Europe and internationally.

Our team combines technical expertise in software development, cybersecurity and intelligence analysis to turn massive volumes of open information into exploitable, sourced and defensible findings.

We operate strictly within a defined legal framework: written mandate, compliance with GDPR and applicable legislation, strict confidentiality of all cases handled.

Legal Framework & Ethics — SovereignMan OÜ acts only under a legitimate mandate, in compliance with Estonian, European (GDPR) and applicable international law. No service is provided outside a verified contractual and legal framework.
